# Launch Kit (zero-accounts edition)

The owner currently has **no accounts** on Reddit / Product Hunt / HN /
forums. That matters: brand-new accounts posting promo links get
auto-flagged as spam (Reddit weights account age + karma heavily). You
**cannot** make an account today and successfully post a link tomorrow.

So distribution is split into two tracks. Do **Track A now** (only needs
your email). Start **Track B aging** in parallel so the social posts in
`docs/DISTRIBUTION.md` actually land in ~3 weeks.

## TRACK A — do now (only needs your Gmail)

### A1. Ham-specific directory: DXZone  ⭐ best first link
DXZone is the big amateur-radio link directory — a relevant, real backlink.
- Go to the Morse category: https://www.dxzone.com/catalog/Operating_Modes/Morse_code/
- Use its "Add your link / Suggest a link". Submit the homepage with the
  short description above. (Also fits: .../Morse_code/Software/ and /Tools/.)

### A2. General free directories (10–15 quality > hundreds of junk)
Submit the listing kit to: **AlternativeTo** (as an alternative to other
Morse translators), **SaaSHub**, **BetaList**. Skip paid "submit to 500
directories" services — low value, spammy.

### A3. Email outreach to CW resource pages ⭐ highest-value no-account move
These pages already curate CW learning links — being added is a real,
topical backlink. Find the webmaster/contact on each site and send the
email below. Lead with their page, not your pitch.

Prospect list (verify each is still live + find the contact):
- Lowell ARC — CW resources: https://w8lrc.org/tutorials/cw-resources/
- CWops — CW resources: https://cwops.org/cw-resources/
- HAM Radio for Non-Techies: https://hamradiofornontechies.com/learning-morse-code-or-cw/
- Long Island CW Club: https://longislandcwclub.org/
- GSARA "Code Buddies": https://www.gsara.org/codebuddies
- Pottstown ARC — CW practice: https://www.paarc.net/cw-practice-and-classes/
- AA9PW practice site: https://www.aa9pw.com/morsecode/

**Outreach email (paste, fill the brackets):**
> Subject: A free CW practice tool for your resources page
>
> Hi [club / name],
>
> I came across your CW resources page ([their URL]) — really useful list.
> I built a free, no-login Morse toolkit your members might like: a Koch
> trainer, a straight-key "keyer" that decodes your sending timing live,

>

> Either way, thanks for keeping that page up.
>
> 73, [your name / callsign]

Send 3–5 a day, personalized. Even 2–3 "yes" replies = real topical links.

### A4. The embed widget (evergreen)
Mention in every email that they can embed the translator free
(https://morsecodegenerator.com/embed/) — it carries a "Powered by" link,
so each embed is a backlink. Teacher/STEM blogs are good targets too.

## TRACK B — start aging now, post in ~3 weeks

Create these accounts **today**, then *participate genuinely* (comment,
upvote, answer questions — no self-promo) until they have some age + karma.
Then post the ready-made content in `docs/DISTRIBUTION.md`.
- **Reddit** (for r/amateurradio, r/morse) — needs the most aging (~2–3 wks, ~50+ comment karma).
- **Product Hunt** — make the maker profile, follow/upvote others first.
- **Hacker News** — account age helps; the Show HN is in DISTRIBUTION.md.
- **QRZ / eHam** — ham forum accounts (a callsign helps credibility).
